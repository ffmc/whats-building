Overview

The palette and the argument are the same thing. The central finding is a polarity — repos are overwhelmingly AI-related or overwhelmingly not, with almost nothing between. That polarity is the brand: signal red reads as hot, urgent, alarm-adjacent — the AI pole; neon chartreuse reads as radioactive, organic-but-synthetic — the not-AI pole. Both run at full saturation everywhere the polarity itself needs to show: buttons, chips, prose.

The diverging chart is deliberately not brand-colored. Red and chartreuse sit close enough on the deuteranopia confusion axis that the dataviz validator collapses them to ΔE 1.4 (floor 6, target 8) — re-lightening one arm doesn't fix it, the hues themselves have to differ. So divergingAI / divergingNotAI run a decoupled, validated indigo/gold pair instead (worst cross-arm ΔE 21.6 deutan). Brand red/chartreuse still carry the polarity everywhere else.

The numbers are the display face. Text is Geist and recedes. Every number is Martian Mono — including hero figures at 168px. On most sites the headline shouts and the data whispers; here 50.2% is the loudest object on screen and the sentence around it is quiet. For a page whose entire argument is quantitative, that is the honest arrangement.

Capital and Driftime were references for behaviour — long decelerating motion, oversized display type, a persistent bottom control bar — and explicitly not for appearance. Both are warm-black with a green accent and a light geometric sans; this system is deliberately none of those.

Sequential — steel, for plain magnitude

C2D3E89CB3D1 7A94B75A769A 415A79
Why steel and not the primary red. Magnitude bars appear in beats that have nothing to do with AI. If counts were drawn in the AI pole's hue, every bar chart would imply an AI reading it doesn't carry. Desaturated steel says "this is just a count," and keeps saturated red and chartreuse meaning one thing only. Untouched by the brand-colour change.

A normal vertical wheel or trackpad gesture moves the rail sideways — the reader should never think about it. Animate transform and opacity only.

Reduced motion is a first-class path, not a fallback. Lenis smoothing off, native scrolling takes over, draw-on skipped, staggers collapsed, hero figure renders its final value. Nothing becomes unreachable and no information is lost.

Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Bind every value to a token; re-run the dataviz validator after any colour change.
  • Let red and chartreuse mean AI and not-AI everywhere — chart, chip, and prose.
  • Set every number in Martian Mono with tabular figures; every sentence in Geist.
  • Use the steel ramp for plain magnitude so counts never imply an AI reading.
  • Reserve elevation.glow for the active beat's primary chart and the hero figure.
  • Keep the record count visible in the control bar at all times.
  • Ship every status message with an icon and a label.
  • Give every chart a table view and every beat a deep link.

Don't

  • Don't add a sixth categorical hue. Fold into "Other" or change the form.
  • Don't put a hue at the diverging midpoint — it is grey #74747A.
  • Don't use the primary red for plain magnitude bars — that is the steel ramp's job.
  • Don't use red or chartreuse for UI emphasis unrelated to the AI polarity.
  • Don't use brand red/chartreuse directly in the diverging Sankey scale — use divergingAI / divergingNotAI.
  • Don't signal an error with colour alone — always pair with an icon and label.
  • Don't set prose in Martian Mono or headline figures in Geist.
  • Don't animate anything but transform and opacity.
  • Don't let the stage rail or control bar overlap content — reserve their height.
  • Don't use warm near-black, a green accent, or a light-weight geometric sans.
  • Don't add gradient text, glassmorphism, or a glowing grid backdrop. The one bold move is the numeric display type at scale, already spent.
  • Don't scale cards on hover or fake elevation with shadow on ground.
